# Electricity Calculator India v1.0

Browser-based electricity bill estimator.

- Units from previous/current kWh meter readings
- Editable progressive tariff slabs
- Fixed monthly charge, billing days, duty/tax and other charges
- Estimated payable total, average cost per unit and slab-wise breakdown
- Add/remove tariff slabs; save tariff locally in browser
- Print estimate; dark/light theme

**Important:** Default rates are illustrative sample values, not official tariffs. Replace them with your latest bill's rates. Tariffs and subsidies vary by state, DISCOM and consumer category. This estimate prorates fixed charges over 30 days and applies duty to energy plus fixed charges. Provider calculations may differ. Free-unit benefits, fuel adjustment, arrears, minimum charges and other special rules are not automatically included.
