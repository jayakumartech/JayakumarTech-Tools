# Smart QR India
Browser-based QR generator for UPI payment links, Wi-Fi, vCard contacts, websites, text, email and phone.

Features: UPI payee/VPA/optional amount/note; Wi-Fi WPA/WEP/open and hidden network; contact vCard; website, text, email and phone; custom QR foreground/background and 256–768 px size; PNG download; copy encoded content; dark/light theme; browser-side generation.

The QR library loads from cdnjs, so internet is required when opening the tool. Test payment QR codes with your UPI app and verify recipient and amount before paying. This tool does not process payments. Avoid encoding confidential information.
